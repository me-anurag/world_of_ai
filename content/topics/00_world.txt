# WORLD + all continents (L0, L1). Unmapped continents are stubs until built.
!defaults d=beg st=foundational

== ai | Artificial Intelligence
def: The field of building machines that perceive, reason, learn and act to achieve goals. It contains symbolic AI, probabilistic AI, evolutionary computing and machine learning, and machine learning contains deep learning.
yr: 1956
paper: McCarthy, Minsky, Rochester, Shannon | A Proposal for the Dartmouth Summer Research Project on Artificial Intelligence | 1955

== hist | History, Philosophy & Framing
def: Where AI came from, what "intelligence" and "agent" mean, the big debates, and the schools of thought that shaped every other field.

== found | Foundations
def: The math and computer science everything else stands on: linear algebra, calculus, probability, statistics, information theory, optimization, numerics, signals, discrete math and CS.

== sym | Symbolic & Classical AI
def: AI by explicit representation and reasoning: search, logic, knowledge representation, planning, constraint satisfaction, expert systems, fuzzy logic.

== prob | Probabilistic & Bayesian AI
def: Reasoning and learning under uncertainty: graphical models, Bayesian inference, sampling and variational methods, Gaussian processes, causal inference, decision theory.

== evo | Evolutionary & Nature-Inspired Computing
def: Optimization by simulated evolution and collective behaviour: genetic algorithms, genetic programming, evolution strategies, swarm intelligence.

== ml | Machine Learning
def: Learning functions from data: paradigms, classical algorithms, learning settings, evaluation and learning theory.

== dl | Deep Learning
def: Machine learning with multi-layer neural networks: training machinery, architectures (CNN, RNN, Transformer, GNN), generative models, representation learning.

== rl | Reinforcement Learning, Games & Multi-Agent Systems
def: Learning to act from reward, plus game theory and multi-agent systems.

== dom | Domains
def: Territories where the methods are applied: NLP, computer vision, speech & audio, information retrieval, multimodal, robotics, time series, graphs, recommenders.

== llm | Large Language Models
def: Transformer language models trained at scale: pretraining, post-training, reasoning, efficiency, model families.

== sys | LLM Systems
def: Systems built on top of LLMs: prompting, RAG, agents, tool use, evaluation, guardrails.

== front | Frontier & AGI
def: Open research territory: AGI, world models, neuro-symbolic AI, cognitive architectures, brain-inspired computing.

== infra | Infrastructure & Practice
def: Data, hardware, frameworks, training at scale, deployment and MLOps.

== safe | Safety, Security, Ethics & Governance
def: Making AI robust, secure, fair, private, interpretable, aligned and governed.

== apps | Application Verticals
def: Where the other fields are applied: medicine, science, finance, games, autonomous driving, industry.
