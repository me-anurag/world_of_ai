######## Speech & audio
== dom.speech | Speech & audio
def: Recognizing, generating and understanding spoken language and other sound: speech recognition, speech synthesis, speaker recognition, audio classification and music.
pre: found.signal.stft
paper: Jurafsky, Martin | Speech and Language Processing | 2000

== dom.speech.features | Acoustic features (MFCCs, mel spectrograms)
def: Turn waveforms into frames of frequency content on a perceptual (mel) scale; MFCCs compress them further.
pre: found.signal.stft
d: beg

== dom.speech.asr | Automatic speech recognition
def: Convert speech to text. Classic systems combined acoustic HMM-GMM models, a pronunciation lexicon and an n-gram language model; neural end-to-end systems replaced them. Measured by word error rate.
pre: dom.speech.features, prob.gm.hmm, dom.nlp.ngram
paper: Hinton et al. | Deep Neural Networks for Acoustic Modeling in Speech Recognition | 2012

== dom.speech.ctc | CTC & end-to-end ASR (RNN-T, attention)
def: Connectionist temporal classification aligns unsegmented audio to text without frame labels; RNN transducers and attention-based encoder–decoders followed.
pre: dom.speech.asr, found.cs.algo.dp
paper: Graves, Fernández, Gomez, Schmidhuber | Connectionist Temporal Classification: Labelling Unsegmented Sequence Data with Recurrent Neural Networks | 2006
d: adv

== dom.speech.ssl | Self-supervised speech (wav2vec 2.0, HuBERT) & Whisper
def: Pretrain on unlabelled audio by masked prediction, or train a Transformer on huge weakly labelled audio (Whisper) for robust multilingual recognition and translation.
pre: dom.speech.ctc, dl.repr.masked
paper: Radford et al. | Robust Speech Recognition via Large-Scale Weak Supervision | 2022
st: dominant

== dom.speech.tts | Text-to-speech
def: Generate natural speech from text: concatenative and parametric systems, then neural acoustic models (Tacotron) with neural vocoders (WaveNet), now codec-token language models and diffusion with voice cloning.
pre: dom.speech.features, dl.gen.autoregressive
paper: Shen et al. | Natural TTS Synthesis by Conditioning WaveNet on Mel Spectrogram Predictions | 2018

== dom.speech.codec | Neural audio codecs & audio tokens
def: Compress audio into discrete tokens (SoundStream, EnCodec) so language models can generate speech and music.
pre: dl.gen.vae.vqvae, dom.speech.features
paper: Zeghidour et al. | SoundStream: An End-to-End Neural Audio Codec | 2021
st: active
d: adv

== dom.speech.speaker | Speaker recognition & diarization
def: Identify who is speaking (speaker embeddings like x-vectors) and segment a recording by speaker.
pre: dom.speech.features, dl.arch.siamese
d: adv

== dom.speech.audio | Audio classification, separation & music AI
aka: keyword spotting; source separation
def: Sound event detection, source separation, music tagging, transcription and music generation.
pre: dom.speech.features, dl.arch.cnn
d: adv

== dom.speech.spoken-dialogue | Spoken dialogue & voice assistants
def: End-to-end voice interfaces combining ASR, an LLM and TTS, increasingly as single speech-to-speech models with low latency.
pre: dom.speech.asr, dom.speech.tts, dom.nlp.dialogue
st: active

######## Information retrieval
== dom.ir | Information retrieval
def: Finding relevant documents in large collections for a query: the science behind search engines, and the "R" in RAG.
pre: ml.data.text
paper: Manning, Raghavan, Schütze | Introduction to Information Retrieval | 2008

== dom.ir.index | Inverted index & Boolean retrieval
def: Map each term to the list of documents containing it so queries touch only relevant postings. The core data structure of search engines.
pre: found.cs.ds.hashmap
d: beg

== dom.ir.vsm | Vector space model & TF-IDF ranking
def: Represent queries and documents as weighted term vectors and rank by cosine similarity.
pre: dom.ir.index, ml.data.text, found.linalg.geom.cosine
paper: Salton, Wong, Yang | A Vector Space Model for Automatic Indexing | 1975
d: beg

== dom.ir.bm25 | BM25
def: Probabilistic ranking function with saturating term frequency and document-length normalization. Still a strong baseline and the sparse half of hybrid RAG search.
f: Σ_t IDF(t)·tf·(k₁+1) / (tf + k₁(1 − b + b·|d|/avgdl))
pre: dom.ir.vsm
paper: Robertson, Zaragoza | The Probabilistic Relevance Framework: BM25 and Beyond | 2009
st: dominant

== dom.ir.eval | IR evaluation (precision@k, recall@k, MAP, MRR, nDCG)
def: Measure ranked results: how many of the top k are relevant, where the first relevant one appears (MRR), graded relevance discounted by position (nDCG).
pre: ml.eval.cls-metrics

== dom.ir.query | Query processing & expansion
def: Spelling correction, synonyms, pseudo-relevance feedback and query rewriting to better match what users mean.
pre: dom.ir.vsm

== dom.ir.pagerank | PageRank & link analysis
def: Score web pages by the stationary distribution of a random surfer following links. Powered Google's early ranking.
pre: found.prob.process.stationary
paper: Brin, Page | The Anatomy of a Large-Scale Hypertextual Web Search Engine | 1998
st: classic

== dom.ir.ltr | Learning to rank in search
def: Combine hundreds of signals with ranking models (LambdaMART) trained on relevance labels or clicks.
pre: dom.ir.eval, ml.settings.ltr

== dom.ir.neural | Neural retrieval (dense, sparse-learned, late interaction)
def: Bi-encoders embed queries and documents for vector search (DPR); learned sparse models (SPLADE) expand terms; late interaction (ColBERT) compares token embeddings.
pre: dom.ir.bm25, dom.nlp.sentence-emb
paper: Karpukhin et al. | Dense Passage Retrieval for Open-Domain Question Answering | 2020
to: sys.rag.retrieval
st: dominant

== dom.ir.rerank | Re-ranking with cross-encoders
def: Score each query–document pair jointly with a Transformer to reorder a shortlist; more accurate than bi-encoders but slower.
pre: dom.ir.neural
paper: Nogueira, Cho | Passage Re-ranking with BERT | 2019
st: dominant

######## Multimodal
== dom.mm | Multimodal learning
def: Models that connect several modalities (text, images, audio, video): matching them, translating between them and reasoning across them.
pre: dom.nlp, dom.cv, dl.repr.multimodal

== dom.mm.captioning | Image captioning & visual question answering
def: Generate descriptions of images or answer questions about them; early systems paired a CNN encoder with an RNN decoder and attention.
pre: dl.arch.attention.cross
paper: Xu et al. | Show, Attend and Tell: Neural Image Caption Generation with Visual Attention | 2015
st: classic

== dom.mm.clip | CLIP
def: Train an image encoder and a text encoder contrastively on hundreds of millions of image–caption pairs so they share an embedding space. Enables zero-shot classification, image search and text conditioning for generators.
pre: dl.repr.contrastive, dl.arch.vit
x: hist.timeline.fm.clip
paper: Radford et al. | Learning Transferable Visual Models From Natural Language Supervision | 2021
st: dominant

== dom.mm.vlm | Vision–language models
def: LLMs that take images (and often video) as input by connecting a vision encoder through a projection or cross-attention (Flamingo, LLaVA, GPT-4V-class models). Read charts, documents and screens.
pre: dom.mm.clip, llm
paper: Liu, Li, Wu, Lee | Visual Instruction Tuning | 2023
to: sys.agents.computer-use
st: dominant

== dom.mm.native | Natively multimodal & any-to-any models
def: Models trained from the start on interleaved text, image, audio and video tokens, able to take and produce several modalities.
pre: dom.mm.vlm, dl.gen.vae.vqvae
st: active
d: adv

== dom.mm.grounding | Visual grounding & referring expressions
def: Link phrases to image regions ("the red cup on the left"), the basis of open-vocabulary detection and GUI agents that click on described elements.
pre: dom.mm.vlm, dom.cv.detection
d: adv

######## Robotics
== dom.robotics | Robotics
def: AI with a body: perceive the physical world, plan and control motion, manipulate objects and move safely around people.
pre: hist.agents, hist.roots.control
paper: Thrun, Burgard, Fox | Probabilistic Robotics | 2005
x: hist.philosophy.mind.moravec

== dom.robotics.kinematics | Kinematics & dynamics
def: Relating joint angles to positions (forward and inverse kinematics) and forces to motion (dynamics).
pre: found.linalg.maps.homogeneous
d: adv

== dom.robotics.localization | Localization (Markov, Monte Carlo localization)
def: Estimate the robot's pose in a known map from noisy sensors using Bayes filters, Kalman filters or particle filters.
pre: prob.temporal.particle

== dom.robotics.slam | SLAM
def: Simultaneous localization and mapping: build a map of an unknown environment while tracking the robot within it (EKF-SLAM, FastSLAM, graph-SLAM).
pre: dom.robotics.localization, dom.cv.geometry.slam
paper: Montemerlo, Thrun, Koller, Wegbreit | FastSLAM: A Factored Solution to the Simultaneous Localization and Mapping Problem | 2002
d: adv

== dom.robotics.motion | Motion planning
def: Find a collision-free path in configuration space: grid search with A*, sampling-based planners (PRM, RRT, RRT*), trajectory optimization.
pre: dom.robotics.kinematics, sym.search.astar
paper: LaValle | Rapidly-Exploring Random Trees: A New Tool for Path Planning | 1998
d: adv

== dom.robotics.control | Control (PID, LQR, MPC)
def: Make the robot follow a planned trajectory despite disturbances: PID feedback, optimal linear control (LQR), model predictive control.
pre: dom.robotics.kinematics, hist.roots.control
x: rl.model.mpc
d: adv

== dom.robotics.manipulation | Grasping & manipulation
def: Picking up and handling objects: grasp detection, contact-rich control, dexterous hands.
pre: dom.robotics.control, dom.cv.3d.pointcloud
d: adv

== dom.robotics.learning | Robot learning (sim-to-real, imitation, RL)
def: Learn skills from demonstrations or RL, often in simulation with domain randomization to transfer to real hardware.
pre: rl.imitation.bc, rl.policy.sac
paper: Tobin et al. | Domain Randomization for Transferring Deep Neural Networks from Simulation to the Real World | 2017
st: active
d: adv

== dom.robotics.foundation | Robot foundation models (vision–language–action)
def: Large models that map camera images and language instructions to robot actions, trained on data pooled across many robots (RT-2, OpenVLA, π0).
pre: dom.robotics.learning, dom.mm.vlm
paper: Brohan et al. | RT-2: Vision-Language-Action Models Transfer Web Knowledge to Robotic Control | 2023
to: front.embodied
st: active
d: adv

== dom.robotics.hri | Human–robot interaction
def: Robots that predict, communicate with and safely work around people, and learn what people want.
x: prob.decision.preferences
d: adv

######## Time series
== dom.ts | Time series & forecasting
def: Modelling data indexed by time (sales, sensors, prices, demand) to forecast, detect anomalies or classify sequences.
pre: found.prob.process
paper: Hyndman, Athanasopoulos | Forecasting: Principles and Practice | 2013

== dom.ts.components | Trend, seasonality, stationarity & autocorrelation
def: Decompose series into trend, seasonal and residual parts; check stationarity and autocorrelation before modelling.
d: beg

== dom.ts.classical | Classical forecasting (exponential smoothing, ARIMA)
def: Exponential smoothing (Holt–Winters) and ARIMA models capture level, trend, seasonality and autocorrelation; strong baselines.
pre: dom.ts.components, ml.sup.linreg
paper: Box, Jenkins | Time Series Analysis: Forecasting and Control | 1970
st: classic

== dom.ts.statespace | State-space forecasting
def: Kalman-filter-based structural models and Prophet-style decomposable models for business forecasting.
pre: dom.ts.classical, prob.temporal.kalman
d: adv

== dom.ts.ml | ML & deep forecasting
def: Gradient boosting on lag features, and neural models (DeepAR, N-BEATS, Temporal Fusion Transformer, PatchTST).
pre: dom.ts.components, ml.ensemble.gbm, dl.arch.transformer
paper: Salinas, Flunkert, Gasthaus, Januschowski | DeepAR: Probabilistic Forecasting with Autoregressive Recurrent Networks | 2020
d: adv

== dom.ts.foundation | Time-series foundation models
def: Large pretrained forecasters that work zero-shot on new series (TimesFM, Chronos). Their advantage over tuned baselines varies by dataset.
pre: dom.ts.ml
st: active
d: adv

== dom.ts.eval | Forecast evaluation & backtesting
def: Rolling-origin evaluation, scale-free errors (MASE, sMAPE) and probabilistic scores; never shuffle time.
pre: ml.eval.cv

######## Graphs
== dom.graph | Graph learning
def: Machine learning on networks: predict node labels, missing links or properties of whole graphs (molecules, social networks, transactions, knowledge graphs).
pre: found.discrete.graphs
paper: Hamilton | Graph Representation Learning | 2020

== dom.graph.spectral | Spectral graph methods
def: Use eigenvectors of the graph Laplacian for clustering, embedding and defining graph convolutions.
pre: found.discrete.graphs.adjacency, found.linalg.decomp.eigen
d: adv

== dom.graph.embedding | Node embeddings (DeepWalk, node2vec)
def: Learn node vectors by running random walks and applying word2vec-style training.
pre: found.prob.process.random-walk, dom.nlp.word2vec
paper: Grover, Leskovec | node2vec: Scalable Feature Learning for Networks | 2016

== dom.graph.tasks | Node classification, link prediction, graph classification
def: The three main graph learning tasks, now usually solved with GNNs.
pre: dl.arch.gnn
x: dom.graph.embedding

== dom.graph.kge | Knowledge graph embeddings
def: Embed entities and relations so true triples score highly (TransE, RotatE), enabling link prediction and knowledge base completion.
pre: dom.graph.embedding, sym.kr.kg
paper: Bordes et al. | Translating Embeddings for Modeling Multi-relational Data | 2013
d: adv

== dom.graph.molecules | Molecular graphs & chemistry
def: Atoms as nodes and bonds as edges for property prediction and molecule generation in drug discovery.
pre: dom.graph.tasks, dl.arch.gnn.equivariant
to: apps.science
d: adv

######## Recommender systems
== dom.recsys | Recommender systems
def: Predict what a user will want next (products, videos, songs, posts). Among the most commercially important uses of ML.
pre: ml.paradigms.supervised
paper: Aggarwal | Recommender Systems: The Textbook | 2016

== dom.recsys.content | Content-based filtering
def: Recommend items similar to what the user liked, using item features or embeddings.
pre: found.linalg.geom.cosine
d: beg

== dom.recsys.cf | Collaborative filtering
def: Recommend what similar users liked (user–user) or items liked by people who liked this item (item–item), from interaction data alone.
pre: ml.sup.knn
d: beg

== dom.recsys.mf | Matrix factorization
def: Factor the sparse user–item rating matrix into low-dimensional user and item vectors; won the Netflix Prize.
f: r̂ᵤᵢ = pᵤᵀqᵢ
pre: dom.recsys.cf, found.linalg.decomp.lowrank
paper: Koren, Bell, Volinsky | Matrix Factorization Techniques for Recommender Systems | 2009

== dom.recsys.implicit | Implicit feedback & ranking losses
def: Learn from clicks and views rather than ratings, with pairwise ranking objectives (BPR) and negative sampling.
pre: dom.recsys.mf, ml.settings.ltr
d: adv

== dom.recsys.deep | Deep & two-tower recommenders
def: Two-tower retrieval models embed users and items for candidate generation; deep ranking models (Wide & Deep, DLRM) score candidates; sequential models (SASRec) use session history.
pre: dom.recsys.implicit, dl.repr.embedding
paper: Covington, Adams, Sargin | Deep Neural Networks for YouTube Recommendations | 2016
st: dominant
d: adv

== dom.recsys.issues | Cold start, popularity bias & feedback loops
def: New users and items lack data; recommenders amplify what is already popular and shape the data they later learn from.
x: safe.fairness
d: beg

######## Tabular data
== dom.tabular | Tabular learning
def: Rows and columns of mixed numeric and categorical data, the most common data in business. Gradient-boosted trees still usually beat deep learning here.
pre: ml.ensemble.xgboost
paper: Grinsztajn, Oyallon, Varoquaux | Why Do Tree-Based Models Still Outperform Deep Learning on Typical Tabular Data? | 2022

== dom.tabular.deep | Deep learning for tables (TabNet, FT-Transformer, TabPFN)
def: Neural architectures and pretrained in-context models for tabular data; TabPFN can beat tuned trees on small datasets.
pre: dl.arch.transformer
st: active
d: adv

######## Anomaly detection applications
== dom.anomaly | Anomaly detection applications
def: Fraud detection, intrusion detection, predictive maintenance and quality control: rare events, heavy imbalance and adaptive adversaries.
pre: ml.unsup.anomaly, ml.data.imbalance

pre: llm
paper: Chen et al. | Evaluating Large Language Models Trained on Code | 2021
to: sys.agents.coding
