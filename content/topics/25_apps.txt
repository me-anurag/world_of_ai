# APPLICATION VERTICALS (shallower by design: shows where each field gets applied)
# Audit: Stanford AI Index (applications chapters), AIMA 1.4 state of the art, major landmark systems per sector.
!defaults d=beg st=active

== apps.science | AI for science
def: AI as a scientific instrument: predicting structures and properties, simulating physics, forecasting weather, proposing hypotheses and proving theorems.
pre: dl.arch.gnn.equivariant
x: front.discovery

== apps.science.alphafold | AlphaFold
def: Predicts a protein's 3D structure from its amino-acid sequence at near-experimental accuracy; its database covers over 200 million proteins. Its creators shared the 2024 Nobel Prize in Chemistry.
pre: dl.arch.transformer
x: hist.timeline.fm.alphafold, hist.timeline.genai.nobel
paper: Jumper et al. | Highly Accurate Protein Structure Prediction with AlphaFold | 2021
yr: 2020
st: milestone

== apps.science.weather | Weather & climate forecasting
def: Learned global weather models (GraphCast, GenCast, Pangu-Weather) rival or beat physics-based forecasts at far lower cost.
pre: dl.arch.gnn
paper: Lam et al. | Learning Skillful Medium-Range Global Weather Forecasting | 2023

== apps.science.materials | Materials, chemistry & drug discovery
def: Predicting molecular and crystal properties, generating candidate drugs and materials, and planning synthesis.
pre: dom.graph.molecules
paper: Merchant et al. | Scaling Deep Learning for Materials Discovery | 2023

== apps.science.math | AI for mathematics
def: AI that proves theorems, finds counterexamples and discovers algorithms; reached IMO gold-medal level in 2025.
pre: sym.logic.provers
x: hist.timeline.genai.imo, evo.llm

== apps.medicine | Medicine & healthcare
def: Medical imaging diagnosis, clinical documentation, risk prediction, triage and drug discovery; constrained by validation, regulation, privacy and fairness.
pre: dom.cv.classification, ml.eval.calibration
paper: Esteva et al. | Dermatologist-level Classification of Skin Cancer with Deep Neural Networks | 2017
x: safe.fairness

== apps.driving | Autonomous driving
def: Perception, prediction, planning and control for self-driving vehicles; driverless taxis operate commercially in several cities, while full general autonomy remains unsolved.
pre: dom.cv.detection, dom.robotics.motion, dom.cv.3d.pointcloud
x: hist.timeline.stat.grandchallenge

== apps.finance | Finance
def: Fraud detection, credit scoring, algorithmic trading, risk modelling and customer-service automation; heavily regulated for fairness and explainability.
pre: dom.anomaly, dom.ts, ml.ensemble.gbm
x: safe.fairness

== apps.games | Games & interactive entertainment
def: Game-playing agents, procedural content generation, AI-driven characters and AI-assisted game development.
pre: rl.games
x: dom.cv.generation

== apps.education | Education
def: Tutoring systems, automated feedback and LLM study assistants; raises questions about assessment and over-reliance.
pre: sys.app
x: safe.ethics.wellbeing

== apps.law | Law & government
def: Legal research, contract review, e-discovery and public-service automation; hallucinated citations have already caused court sanctions.
pre: sys.rag
x: llm.behavior.hallucination

== apps.software | Software engineering
def: Code completion, coding agents, automated testing and code review; the fastest-adopted professional use of LLMs.
pre: sys.agents.coding

== apps.business | Business operations, customer service & marketing
def: Chat assistants, document processing, analytics, search, personalization and recommendation across enterprises.
pre: sys.rag, dom.recsys

== apps.creative | Creative industries
def: Image, video, music and text generation for design, film, advertising and writing; contested over copyright and labour.
pre: dom.cv.generation, dom.speech.tts
x: safe.ethics.creative

== apps.industry | Manufacturing, logistics & energy
def: Visual inspection, predictive maintenance, demand forecasting, route optimization, robotics in warehouses and grid optimization.
pre: dom.anomaly, dom.ts, sym.csp.optimization

== apps.agriculture | Agriculture & environment
def: Crop and pest detection from images, yield prediction, precision farming and satellite monitoring of land and climate.
pre: dom.cv.classification, dom.ts

== apps.cyber | Cybersecurity
def: AI for intrusion and malware detection and for finding vulnerabilities, and the same capabilities used by attackers.
pre: dom.anomaly
x: safe.llm.misuse
