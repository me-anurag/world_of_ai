# Final gap-audit additions (ACM Computing Classification System: AI + ML branches; arXiv cs.AI/LG/CL/CV/RO/MA/NE/stat.ML scopes; Szeliski ch.10; SLP3 morphology; recent LLM practice)
!defaults d=int st=foundational

== sym.kr.abduction | Abduction & model-based diagnosis
def: Reasoning to the best explanation of observations (the fault that explains the symptoms). Model-based diagnosis finds which components must be faulty given a system model.
pre: sym.logic.fol
paper: Reiter | A Theory of Diagnosis from First Principles | 1987
d: adv

== sym.kr.argumentation | Computational argumentation
def: Represent arguments and attacks between them and compute which arguments survive (Dung's frameworks); used for reasoning with conflicting information and in explainable AI.
pre: sym.kr.nonmono
paper: Dung | On the Acceptability of Arguments and its Fundamental Role in Nonmonotonic Reasoning, Logic Programming and n-Person Games | 1995
d: adv

== ml.sup.rules | Rule learning (RIPPER, CN2)
def: Learn ordered or unordered sets of IF–THEN rules directly from data by sequential covering; interpretable alternatives to trees.
pre: ml.sup.trees
paper: Cohen | Fast Effective Rule Induction | 1995
st: classic

== ml.sup.survival | Survival analysis
def: Predict time until an event (death, churn, failure) when many examples are censored (the event hasn't happened yet): Kaplan–Meier, Cox proportional hazards, survival forests.
pre: ml.sup.glm, found.prob.dist.exponential
paper: Cox | Regression Models and Life-Tables | 1972
d: adv

== ml.eval.conformal | Conformal prediction
def: Wrap any model to output prediction sets or intervals with a guaranteed coverage rate (e.g., 90%), assuming only exchangeable data.
pre: ml.eval.calibration, found.stats.testing.ci
paper: Angelopoulos, Bates | A Gentle Introduction to Conformal Prediction and Distribution-Free Uncertainty Quantification | 2021
st: active
d: adv

== ml.theory.grokking | Grokking & delayed generalization
def: Networks sometimes generalize long after they have memorized the training set, a phenomenon studied to understand how learning dynamics form general algorithms.
pre: ml.theory.deep
paper: Power, Burda, Edwards, Babuschkin, Misra | Grokking: Generalization Beyond Overfitting on Small Algorithmic Datasets | 2022
st: active
d: adv

== dom.nlp.morphology | Morphology & linguistic structure
def: How words are built from smaller meaningful parts (prefixes, roots, inflections), which matters for richly inflected languages and tokenizer design.
pre: dom.nlp.text
d: beg

== dom.nlp.corpora | Corpora, treebanks & language resources
def: Annotated text collections and lexicons (Penn Treebank, Universal Dependencies, Common Crawl derivatives) that NLP is trained and evaluated on.
paper: Marcus, Santorini, Marcinkiewicz | Building a Large Annotated Corpus of English: The Penn Treebank | 1993
d: beg

== dom.cv.retrieval | Image retrieval & visual search
def: Find images similar to a query image or text using global embeddings and nearest-neighbour search; instance retrieval and reverse image search.
pre: dom.cv.foundation, sys.rag.ann

== dom.cv.compphoto | Computational photography
def: Combine multiple exposures or algorithms to beat optics: HDR, panoramas, denoising bursts, portrait-mode depth effects, as in phone cameras.
pre: dom.cv.geometry.homography, dom.cv.restoration
paper: Hasinoff et al. | Burst Photography for High Dynamic Range and Low-Light Imaging on Mobile Cameras | 2016
d: adv

== dom.cv.biometrics | Biometrics (face, fingerprint, iris)
def: Recognizing people by physical traits; raises accuracy, spoofing, privacy and fairness issues.
pre: dom.cv.face
x: safe.ethics.surveillance
d: beg

== llm.eff.merging | Model merging & task arithmetic
def: Combine fine-tuned models by averaging or adding their weight differences, getting multi-skill models without more training.
pre: llm.eff.lora
paper: Ilharco et al. | Editing Models with Task Arithmetic | 2022
st: active
d: adv

== llm.pretrain.collapse | Model collapse
def: Training repeatedly on model-generated data can lose the tails of the original distribution and degrade models; mixing in real data avoids it.
pre: llm.pretrain.synthetic
paper: Shumailov et al. | AI Models Collapse when Trained on Recursively Generated Data | 2024
st: debate
d: adv
